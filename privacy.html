Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Package: com.encrypz.privacy

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture & Encrypz Vault V2

Encrypz is engineered on a 100% client-side, zero-knowledge architecture powered by Encrypz Vault V2 (EPZ2). All key derivation, filename obfuscation, and file encryption and decryption execute locally inside your Android device's sandbox. Master keys are wrapped using memory-hard RFC 7914 scrypt (N=32768, r=8, p=1 with a 16-byte random salt) and RFC 3394 AES Key Wrap, authenticated with KDF-bound HMAC-SHA256 manifests (vault.encrypz), filenames and paths are encrypted with domain-separated RFC 5297 AES-256-SIV, and file contents are encrypted in streaming 64 KiB AES-256-GCM chunks before ever reaching storage or cloud sync.

2. Zero Backend Servers & User-Controlled Storage

Encrypz operates completely serverless with no Encrypz backend servers, no external database, and no user account registration server. Offline vaults stay strictly on your Android device, and optional Google Drive vaults synchronize directly between your phone and your personal Google Drive account.

We believe in absolute transparency regarding what encrypted artifacts remain under your control versus what Encrypz never sees or collects:

Stored Only on Your Device / Drive

User-Controlled
  • Encrypted Vault Metadata (vault.encrypz & masterkey.encrypz): scrypt-wrapped master keys and HMAC-SHA256 signed manifest stored solely inside your chosen phone folder or Google Drive vault.
  • Encrypted File Blobs (vault/XX/YYYY): EPZ2 64 KiB AES-256-GCM encrypted file chunks alongside AES-256-SIV obfuscated filenames (.epz / .epzl).
  • Android Keystore Quick-Unlock Material: Hardware/OS-backed sealed credentials stored strictly on-device when optional 6-digit PIN or biometric unlock is enabled (Android cloud auto-backup is disabled).
  • Local Google OAuth Session (Optional): Stored locally only if you create or open a Google Drive vault, enabling direct device-to-Drive API requests without any Encrypz relay server.

NEVER Collected by Encrypz

Zero Server Access
  • No Encrypz Servers or Databases: Encrypz runs no account backend, database, or file proxy; nothing is transmitted to Encrypz infrastructure.
  • Plaintext Passwords, PINs & Recovery Keys: Never leave your device and are never logged, transmitted, or stored in plaintext anywhere.
  • File Contents & Plaintext Filenames: Every byte and filename is encrypted locally before being written to device storage or synced to Google Drive.
  • Decryption Keys, Telemetry & Analytics: Active vault keys exist only in volatile memory while unlocked and are wiped immediately on lock; zero IP logs, cookies, ads, or analytics SDKs.

3. Offline Vaults & Direct Google Drive Transfers

Encrypz supports completely offline vaults in Phone Storage and any user-selected Folder on Phone (via Android Storage Access Framework), which operate with zero network traffic or Google sign-in. If you choose to create or open a Google Drive vault, Encrypz authenticates directly via official Google OAuth 2.0 (native Android authorization with PKCE loopback fallback) and transfers encrypted chunks directly between your device and Google Drive REST v3 using resumable uploads with zero middleman servers.

4. Streaming Chunked Encryption (Up to 4 GB)

Encrypz Vault V2 (EPZ2) processes files using constant-memory streaming I/O in 64 KiB AES-256-GCM authenticated chunks with per-file random content keys and chunk-index AAD authentication. This allows importing, encrypting, exporting plaintext copies to user-chosen destinations, and performing random-access encrypted video and audio playback for files up to 4 GB per file (up to 30 files per batch) without loading large files into RAM.

5. Device Protection: Biometrics, PIN, FLAG_SECURE & Immediate Locking

Vaults are protected by your strong master password (12+ characters recommended), with optional 6-digit PIN quick unlock and Android Biometric / Fingerprint unlock sealed by the Android Keystore. For physical and screen privacy, Encrypz enforces immediate locking whenever the app moves to the background (purging active keys from memory) and enables FLAG_SECURE by default to block screenshots and hide previews in the Android Recents switcher. Encrypted photos are sorted chronologically by creation date in a built-in Photos timeline without exposing metadata to cloud providers.

6. Vault Restoration & Offline Recovery Key

Because Encrypz enforces strict zero-knowledge and serverless principles, you remain in full control of your encrypted data across device resets and reinstalls. Reopen any portable Folder on Phone vault via Select Existing Vault on Phone, or sign into your Google account and tap Open Existing Google Drive Vault to locate vault.encrypz and unlock with your master password. If you forget your master password, tap Recover with Recovery Key and use your offline CRC-16/CCITT checksummed Base32 Recovery Key to reset it offline without re-encrypting your files. If you lose both your master password and your offline Base32 Recovery Key, Encrypz cannot recover your keys or decrypt your data.

7. Data Collection & Analytics

Encrypz collects zero personal or usage data. We operate no backend servers, never log IP addresses, never use tracking cookies, and never bundle third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

8. Open Source Transparency

Encrypz is open-source under package name com.encrypz.privacy at github.com/Encrypz/EncrypzAndroid. We welcome independent security researchers and users to audit our cryptographic implementation and verify all privacy claims.
